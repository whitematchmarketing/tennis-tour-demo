How the tour works

Everything happens online. Pick your level, enter, play, and report your own scores. No forms to email, no waiting on anyone.

1

Pick your level

Juniors, Futures, Challengers, ATP/WTA or Masters. You can enter events at your level, one below, or one above.

2

Enter a tournament

€15 each, four players in a round robin. New events are posted every Saturday morning.

3

Play your match

You get your opponents' contacts each week and one week to play. Two sets to 6 games, tiebreak to 10 if level.

4

Report your score

Submit the result online. It counts straight away, your opponent is notified and can dispute it if needed.

5

Climb the rankings

Points count for a full year and update every Wednesday. Finish 1st in five events at your level to move up.

6

Earn on activity

Play more to earn badges and activity points on the leaderboard. The more you play, the more you earn.

Organiser tools

Why it helps: the four-player groups and the rankings build themselves from who enters and who wins, so you stop emailing opponents and editing sheets.

Create a tournament

Publish it and the round-robin groups build themselves from who enters.

Manage tournaments

Reschedule a tournament: push the start if a group has not filled, or set the real dates once all 4 players are in. Players get the new schedule.

Match results

Results appear here the moment a player reports them. If a player tells you offline, log it yourself. Resolve any disputes with one tap.

Tournament entries

Players stay pending until you confirm their €15 payment. Approve to release their group and opponents’ contacts. Switch off to let players in automatically.

New registrations

Approve a player and add them to their level.

Rankings auto

Rankings calculate themselves from match results, nobody uploads them. Use a manual adjustment only for imported history, strike penalties, or a correction.

Players & members

Everyone who has signed up. Search the list, add a player by hand, or bulk-import the CSV you export from your Google Form responses (Google Forms: Responses → open in Sheets → File → Download → Comma-separated values).

GDPR: as organiser you are the data controller. Import only details people agreed to share. Remove honours a deletion request; Export CSV honours a data request.
